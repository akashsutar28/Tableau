About the Project

Electric Vehicle Analysis in India is a data analytics project that explores the rapid growth of electric mobility using interactive data visualizations.

EV Market Insights

Analyze trends in electric vehicle adoption, pricing patterns, and market growth across India.

Vehicle Performance

Compare EV specifications such as driving range, battery capacity, and efficiency across different brands.

Charging Infrastructure

Explore charging station distribution and understand the availability of EV infrastructure in India.

Technology Used

  • Tableau – Data Visualization and Dashboard Creation
  • Python Flask – Web Application Framework
  • HTML, CSS, Bootstrap – Frontend Design
  • Electric Vehicle Dataset – Market and Vehicle Data Analysis
